## Full description

Turn a compatible Android device into a digital signage player or menu board for your shop, cafe, office or event. Show photos and videos, build playlists and manage your screen from a browser on the same local network.

Local Signage keeps your media on the device. Downloaded local photos and videos can keep playing without an internet connection. No app account is required to manage local content.

LOCAL MEDIA PLAYBACK
Upload images and videos for promotions, menus, announcements and welcome screens. Arrange content into scenes and playlists for repeat playback.

BROWSER CONTROL ON YOUR LOCAL NETWORK
Connect your Android player and computer or phone to the same local network. Open the address shown by the app in your browser to upload media and manage content.

CONTENT FOR EVERYDAY DISPLAYS
Create photo and video playlists for retail offers, cafe menus, office notices and event information. Keep local content available on the player when the internet is unavailable.

MORE WITH PRO
Pro unlocks advanced content and multi-device management. Add web content, live streams and text, and manage multiple players on your local network. Online content requires a network connection.

GET STARTED
1. Install Local Signage on a compatible Android device.
2. Connect your player and controller to the same local network.
3. Open the player's browser address, upload media and create a playlist.

TRIAL AND SUBSCRIPTION
Start with a 7-day full-feature trial without adding a payment method. After the trial, the free tier supports up to 10 image or video resources and one basic playlist. Existing content is retained and playback continues when the trial ends.

Pro is available as an annual auto-renewing subscription through Google Play. The price is shown before purchase. Manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play. Purchases and periodic subscription verification require an internet connection.

Local media playback works offline. Browser management requires a local network, and web content and live streams depend on their source and network availability. Playback compatibility depends on your device and media format.
