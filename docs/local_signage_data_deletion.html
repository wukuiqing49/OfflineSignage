Account Deletion & User Data Erasure Policy

Effective Date: August 18, 2026 | Last Updated: August 19, 2026
📌 Account-Free & Local-First Architecture Statement Local Signage is an offline-first application that does not require user account registration. Because no central accounts or cloud databases are maintained, you do not need to submit an account deletion request. All your uploaded media, playlists, device settings, and operational logs reside exclusively on your local Android hardware and can be deleted immediately by you at any time.

1. How to Delete Uploaded Media and Playlists

1 Remove Content via the Local Web Console

You can manage and delete individual or multiple media assets directly through the local browser console:

  1. Connect your browser to the Local Signage Web Console (e.g., http://<device-ip>:8080).
  2. Navigate to the Resources / Devices section.
  3. Select the images, videos, texts, or HTML layouts you wish to remove and click Delete.
  4. The media files and database indices will be permanently and immediately removed from the device storage.

2. How to Completely Erase All Application Data

2 Wipe Device Storage via Android Settings

If you are decommissioning a display, transferring hardware, or wish to perform a complete data wipe:

  1. Open your Android device's Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps & Notifications > See all apps > Local Signage.
  3. Select Storage & Cache.
  4. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear All Data).
  5. Alternatively, you can Uninstall the Local Signage application from your device.

Note: Clearing storage or uninstalling the app permanently purges all local media, paired device encryption keys, playback logs, and local preferences with immediate effect. This action is irreversible.

3. Google Play Purchase & Subscription Management

All in-app purchases and subscriptions are securely processed and retained by Google Play:

  • Clearing local application data or uninstalling the app does not automatically cancel active subscriptions.
  • To cancel or manage your subscription, please open the Google Play Store app > tap your profile icon > Payments & Subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  • Purchase receipts and transaction records are associated with your Google Account and are subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention Summary Table

账号注销与用户数据删除说明

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 18 日 | 最近更新：2026 年 8 月 19 日
📌 无账号与本地优先架构说明 Local Signage 为本地离线型广告机工具，无需注册任何应用账号。由于系统未在云端建立用户中心或账号数据库，因此您无需经过复杂的人工注销审核流程。您上传的所有媒体文件、播放方案、设备配置及运行日志均 100% 留存在本地硬件中，您可随时自主、彻底地清除全部数据。

一、如何删除已上传的内容与播放方案

1 通过 Web 控制台精确删除

在日常使用中，如需删除特定的海报、视频或网页内容：

  1. 在局域网电脑或手机浏览器中打开 Local Signage 控制台（如 http://<设备IP>:8080）。
  2. 进入“设备 / 资源”管理页面。
  3. 选中不再需要展示的素材或播放列表，点击“删除”。
  4. 对应的媒体物理文件和数据库记录将立即从设备存储中永久销毁。

二、如何彻底清除设备上的全部应用数据

2 通过 Android 系统设置重置清除

如需更换大屏用途、归还硬件或彻底清除所有使用痕迹：

  1. 打开 Android 系统的“设置 (Settings)”。
  2. 进入“应用与通知” > “查看全部应用” > “Local Signage”。
  3. 点击“存储和缓存 (Storage & Cache)”。
  4. 点击“清除存储空间 / 清除所有数据 (Clear Storage)”。
  5. 或者直接在桌面长按应用图标选择“卸载应用”。

注意：清除存储空间或卸载应用将物理抹除设备上的所有媒体资源、播放记录、局域网配对凭据及试用状态，且无法撤销或恢复。

三、Google Play 订单与订阅数据说明

专业版授权与订阅均由 Google Play 官方结算系统托管：

  • 清除设备数据或卸载应用不会自动终止正在扣费的订阅。
  • 如需取消或管理自动续订，请打开 Google Play 商店 App > 点击右上角头像 > 付款和订阅 > 订阅 进行退订操作。
  • 历史账单与交易明细归属于您的 Google 账号，受 Google 官方隐私保护。
