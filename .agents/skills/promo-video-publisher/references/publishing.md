# Publishing, recovery and verification

## Load only the relevant platform

- [YouTube and Shorts](platforms/youtube.md)
- [TikTok](platforms/tiktok.md)
- [Instagram and Reels](platforms/instagram.md)
- [Threads](platforms/threads.md)

For other platforms, discover the actual fields and capabilities using current official sources and the visible client. Platform references are operational prompts, not permanent guarantees. Record the date, source and relevant account/client observation when verifying a changeable capability.

## Prepare the actual delivery

Use the authorized account, device and task mode. Confirm product identity and the exact existing post before editing. Identify the video through its path, duration, preview and revision; use a checksum when ambiguity warrants it. Transfer the verified file and refresh the media index as needed.

Prepare complete platform-appropriate copy, cover, text and audio before submission. Check whether the actual uploaded video contains the intended overlays or needs a supported caption track. Existing footage receives the same check; an explicit no-text preference is a valid recorded choice. A subtitle file on disk, a cover or a description does not prove text appears in the video. Follow [music](music.md) for track rights, local versus in-app application, and audible playback; carry an established music request across the requested platforms.

Check audience, visibility, language and applicable promotional-content declarations based on the real relationship. Do not enable paid promotion or advertising permissions incidentally. A draft request must remain a draft.

## Identity and destination preflight

Before writing the CTA, confirm the product's official name, developer/publisher name and exact destination from the target project and store/site. Do not substitute the social account handle for the store developer name without evidence. Reuse verified campaign facts; never hardcode a previous product, developer, package or phone into a new campaign.

For app promotions, prefer **product name + developer name + destination URL**, in that order. Put the identity and primary action before long benefit copy, hashtags or credits. Keep the URL on a separate line when the field supports it. For example, using placeholders rather than real campaign facts:

> {Official app name} · {Verified developer name}
> Search “{Official app name}” on Google Play and choose the app by {Verified developer name}.
> https://{verified destination}

Adapt the example to the real operating system and route. When producing an ending card or explanatory overlays, include readable product identity and the same primary action; editing copy alone does not authorize altering existing media. A developer name alone is insufficient to identify one app in a multi-product account.

Inspect the exact platform, content type, account eligibility, available fields and current official guidance. Record which route is primary and its evidence before submission:

| Observed capability | Primary CTA | Supporting treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A clickable body/description link is supported | Product + developer/publisher + direct destination | Verify the actual saved link and redirects; do not require a duplicate download comment |
| Body/comments accept URL text but do not make it clickable | Accurate store/site search instruction, with product + developer | Keep a canonical URL as a copyable fallback when useful; never label it “tap to download” |
| Only profile, Story, related-video or another placement supports links | Use only an available, verified and authorized route | Honor profile preferences; do not say “link in bio” when no matching link exists |
| Capability is absent, unclear or blocked | Best verified alternative, or explicitly unverified route | Record missing eligibility/field or failure; do not repeatedly post URLs to force support |

Prefer a verified canonical URL for manual-copy fallbacks when long tracking parameters obscure the identity/CTA. Record attribution loss; retain tracking when requested or needed for the campaign, using a suitable verified clickable placement where possible. Do not use an unverified shortener. A visually shortened link label is not proof its underlying destination was lost.

## QR and store presentation

When ordinary URLs are not clickable, prefer an available native clickable placement; otherwise show the exact store search terms and verified developer. A QR code is a supporting route, especially for a second screen or screenshot scanning; it does not make the video clickable or guarantee same-phone scanning. Do not promise universal deep links, redirects, shorteners or special text tricks bypass platform restrictions.

Generate QR codes programmatically from the verified HTTPS destination, never with image/video generation. Preserve square modules and a quiet zone, use strong contrast and enough display time, and keep it clear of captions and platform controls. Decode frames from the final encoded video, including a representative reduced/compressed version, and compare the complete payload. Verify the destination separately; neither a successful decode nor a working URL proves installation or conversion.

For an app already live on Google Play, use real store identity and the official download badge from current Google brand resources. Optional real store footage can establish availability; it is not mandatory for every video. Composite badges, QR codes and precise text in post-production, preserve badge proportions/clear space and distinguish download from preregistration. Never generate a fake store listing, ranking, reviews, ratings, install counts or endorsement. Give the ending enough readable time for the chosen CTA, without imposing one fixed duration on all edits. Clearly distinguish illustrative AI scenes from real app recordings and apply the platform's applicable disclosure.

## Requested likes

A publishing request alone does not add engagement actions. If the user asks to like the in-scope posts, inspect each confirmed post and its current like state. Like once with the authorized current account only when it is unliked; leave an existing like selected and verify the saved state. Do not repeatedly toggle an uncertain control, like unrelated content, switch accounts, buy engagement or imply self-likes improve distribution. Record requested, already liked, newly liked or unavailable separately from publication.

## Description and author comment

Prepare the main description/post body using the selected route. Prepare an author comment/reply when the user requested it, license credits require it, or it adds useful information. Do not default to repeating the same full URL in both placements on every platform. If the user explicitly requested both placements or a URL in each, preserve that instruction; explain and record nonclickability instead of silently omitting the URL. On Threads an author reply belongs under the confirmed original post, not in a second top-level promotional post.

Post comments only within the authorized scope. Reuse an explicit request for both placements across the named platforms without asking again. After a new post is confirmed, add the requested prepared author comment; for an existing post, inspect and reuse or edit its author comment before creating another. Existing viewer comments mean it may not be chronologically first; never delete others' comments or claim a fixed ranking. “See description” does not replace an explicitly requested comment URL.

Treat saved URL text, discoverability before/after expansion, owner visibility, visitor visibility, clickability, correct destination and pinning as separate results. Pin only when requested and available; check existing pins before replacing them. Preserve prepared copy if comments are disabled, URLs are rejected or limits prevent completion. Complete independent authorized items and record the exact missing placement. Do not infer shadowbanning from a folded, nonclickable or owner-only-tested comment.

## Submit and read back

Immediately before submission, verify account, file, full text, cover, captions/overlays, music or explicit silent choice, audience and visibility. Existing in-scope authorization is sufficient; do not ask for redundant permission.

After submission, reopen the target post and inspect the full description, cover, text display and audible music. If an author comment/reply is in scope, reopen its area and inspect its full text separately. Obtain a stable share URL where possible and verify that it points to the intended post; never fabricate an ID.

Distinguish submitted, processing, published to the current account and verified externally accessible. An owner-visible post or comment does not prove visitor visibility. Check an external view when available and relevant; otherwise record that limit. Do not turn a platform preview into a claim of complete playback verification.

## Verify the destination

Follow the actual CTA:
- Clickable route: follow the link through redirects to the correct product page.
- Copyable URL: copy it into a browser and verify the destination, while labeling the original placement as nonclickable.
- Search route: use the stated store/site and exact terms, then verify the resulting product and developer identity. A working direct URL does not validate search results, ranking or availability in other countries.

Respect the user's choice not to use profile links. Keep the profile's developer or multi-product identity; only change profile fields when authorized. A working destination does not prove attribution, installation or conversion. Validate desktop and mobile separately when requested; otherwise identify the verified environment.

## Recovery and resumption

Use [the status template](../assets/publishing-status-template.md) to preserve confirmed state. On continuation, read it, confirm the account/post and inspect the current remote state before mutating it. Do not repeat completed publication or comments based solely on an interrupted tool call.

After timeouts, UI read failures, ANRs or navigation changes, inspect posts and drafts before retrying. Use fresh screenshots/UI state; if a hierarchy dump fails or remains stale, do not treat it as evidence for the current screen. Read back pasted or typed copy before submission: input methods can alter names, punctuation and URLs. Clear/discard only the test draft created by this task, including attached previews, without touching pre-existing drafts. During processing, perform a small number of spaced checks while finishing independent work; stop ineffective retries after repeated unchanged results. Schedule later monitoring only when requested.

If another task or person changes the device's foreground, stop input and confirm the intended app, account and fresh page before resuming. Do not continue a queued sequence of taps or text entry against an unexpected application; coordinate access when contention persists.

For a policy rejection, report the rejected operation and known capabilities. Do not evade it by wrapping commands, changing tools or using another entrance. Continue only independent authorized operations that are permitted. A single rejection does not establish that every device operation is unavailable.

For a missing field, use an appropriate available client consistent with the requested device. Do not assume browser login and native-app login share a session. State the specific missing capability without repeatedly requesting unrelated authentication.

When existing video text is missing, first check whether the platform supports adding captions or text to that post. Do not assume the video file is replaceable. If a new upload is necessary, prepare the reviewed revision first and obtain any missing replacement authorization. Hiding or deleting the original requires its own scope. A skill edit never triggers a repost.

Record completed items, exact blockers, pending copy/assets and the next action. Do not use success in one placement or platform to mark all delivery complete.
