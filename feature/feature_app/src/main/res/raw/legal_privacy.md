1. About the product

Local Signage is a local-first signage player. It does not require a Local Signage account and does not collect account passwords. Content management normally happens between the display and a controller on the same local network.

2. Data processed locally

The app stores locally the images, videos, live-stream addresses, web pages, and HTML label content you add, along with playback configuration, device name, pairing state, and runtime settings. To provide local-network control, it processes local network addresses, a device identifier, pairing codes, and necessary diagnostic information. A paired controller on the local network can access and manage content on this device.

3. Google services

Purchases and license verification use Google Play Billing. Google handles payment accounts, orders, and payment methods under its own policies; this app does not receive complete payment details. The project includes Firebase components, but Firebase Analytics is disabled by default. Advertising is not enabled, and the app does not intentionally use advertising identifiers.

4. Permission use

Network access supports local control, live streams, web playback, and Google Play license verification. Nearby-device, Wi-Fi, and location-related permissions are used only to discover or inspect the local network or start the device hotspot, depending on the Android version. Media is processed when you select or upload content.

5. Retention and deletion

Local content remains until you delete it in the console, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Google Play retains purchase records. See Local Data Deletion for instructions.

6. Security

Use local control only on a trusted network and protect pairing codes. The app does not intentionally publish its local control API as a public internet service.

7. Children

This product is intended for signage management and is not directed to children.
