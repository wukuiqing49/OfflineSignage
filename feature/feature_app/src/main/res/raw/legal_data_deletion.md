Local Signage does not create an app account, so there is no Local Signage account to close.

Delete local content: remove uploaded resources and related playback configuration in the Web console.

Delete all app data on this device: open Android Settings, go to Apps > Local Signage > Storage, and clear data, or uninstall the app. This removes device media, configuration, pairing information, and the local trial record and normally cannot be undone.

Google Play purchase records: Google retains orders and subscriptions. Clearing app data or uninstalling does not remove orders and does not cancel a subscription. Manage subscriptions under Payments and subscriptions in Google Play, and use Google Play order support for purchase records or refund requests.

DevHub statistics: turn off Usage statistics in Legal and privacy to stop new requests and clear pending local statistics. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the local installation identifier and consent setting, but does not delete records already received by DevHub. For assistance deleting server records, contact the developer support email below. The Usage statistics switch controls DevHub only.
